Version: 1.0  
Status: Foundation Specification  
Project: Identity Forge (IFORGE)

---

# Purpose

No historical character should become canonical after a single generation.

Identity Forge is built upon iterative refinement.

Every generated image is treated as a hypothesis.

The system analyzes the hypothesis, identifies strengths and weaknesses, proposes targeted improvements, and generates the next iteration.

This continues until the character satisfies the Identity Forge standards.

The objective is not simply to generate images.

The objective is to converge toward truth.

---

# Core Philosophy

Great character design is not created in one prompt.

It is discovered through disciplined iteration.

Every refinement cycle should answer one question:

> "Did this image become more historically faithful, more emotionally engaging, and more consistent with the Identity Forge Universe?"

If the answer is yes,

continue.

If the answer is no,

identify why,

then refine again.

---

# The Refinement Loop

Identity Forge follows a closed feedback loop.

```
Reference Material
        │
        ▼
Initial Generation
        │
        ▼
Evaluation
        │
        ▼
Critique
        │
        ▼
Improvement Plan
        │
        ▼
Revised Generation
        │
        ▼
Comparison
        │
        ▼
Approval
        │
        ▼
Canonical Character
```

Every iteration should have a measurable objective.

---

# Refinement Goals

Each iteration should attempt to improve one or more of the following:

Identity

Historical accuracy

Child appeal

Visual consistency

Emotional readability

Material realism

Lighting

Composition

Expression

Artifact removal

Never attempt to improve everything simultaneously.

Small targeted improvements produce more stable convergence.

---

# Evaluation Categories

Every generated image should be evaluated across the following dimensions.

---

## Identity

Questions:

Does this still look like the historical person?

Has facial geometry drifted?

Have identity anchors been preserved?

Would someone recognize this person from previous images?

---

## Historical Accuracy

Questions:

Does clothing match the period?

Are props historically appropriate?

Has modern styling appeared?

Are colors historically plausible?

Has reconstruction respected the available evidence?

---

## Child Appeal

Questions:

Would a child want to spend time with this character?

Does the character feel human and emotionally readable?

Are the eyes emotionally engaging?

Is the expression authentic to the person?

Is the emotional signature distinct from the rest of the cast?

Does the character feel alive?

---

## Universe Consistency

Questions:

Would this character belong beside every other Identity Forge character?

Does lighting match the universe?

Does stylization remain consistent?

Do proportions remain compatible?

---

## Technical Quality

Questions:

Are materials believable?

Has artifact removal succeeded?

Is anatomy correct?

Are hands correct?

Are eyes symmetrical?

Are there AI artifacts?

---

# The Critique Phase

Identity Forge separates evaluation from generation.

Generation creates.

Critique analyzes.

The critique should never simply state:

"Looks good."

Instead, critique should identify:

Strengths

Weaknesses

Confidence

Priority improvements

Example:

Strengths

Excellent facial identity.

Strong lighting.

Authentic expression.

Distinct emotional signature.

Weaknesses

Hair texture remains too painterly.

Eyes slightly oversized.

Background too busy.

Priority

Reduce hair stylization.

Simplify environment.

Increase beard realism.

---

# Improvement Instructions

The refinement engine converts critique into specific improvements.

Poor refinement instruction:

"Make it better."

Good refinement instruction:

Reduce eye size by approximately five percent.

Preserve facial geometry.

Increase beard strand realism.

Remove remaining engraving texture from forehead.

Warm skin tones slightly.

Simplify laboratory background.

Specific refinement produces predictable improvement.

---

# Refinement Strategy

Identity Forge follows a "one major improvement per iteration" philosophy.

Iteration 1

Iteration 2

Iteration 3

Historical clothing

Iteration 4

Lighting

Iteration 5

Expression

Iteration 6

# Comparison Method

Identity Forge never compares an image against memory.

It compares images side by side.

Questions include:

Which image better preserves identity?

Which image feels more alive?

Which image is more historically accurate?

Which image fits the universe better?

Which image would children remember?

The winning characteristics move forward.

The weaker characteristics are discarded.

---

# Convergence

The objective is convergence.

Each iteration should reduce uncertainty.

Identity should become stronger.

Historical confidence should increase.

Visual consistency should improve.

Refinement should eventually plateau.

When improvements become negligible,

the character is ready for approval.

---

# Regression Detection

Sometimes an iteration becomes worse.

Examples

Identity drift.

Lighting becomes colder.

Expression becomes emotionally flat or inaccessible.

Historical clothing becomes inaccurate.

The refinement engine must detect regression.

If regression occurs,

return to the previous approved version.

Never continue building upon a degraded image.

---

# Human-in-the-Loop

Identity Forge is designed to augment artistic judgment—not replace it.

The final approval remains human.

The human art director decides when:

Identity is correct.

Historical evidence has been respected.

Child appeal is sufficient.

Universe consistency has been achieved.

The refinement engine assists.

It does not replace creative leadership.

Identity similarity

Color palette consistency

Proportion consistency

Lighting consistency

Artifact detection

Material realism

Expression readability

These evaluations support—not replace—human review.

---

# Stopping Criteria

Refinement should stop when:

Identity is stable.

No significant historical improvements remain.

Universe consistency has been achieved.

Child appeal is high.

Artifact removal is complete.

Technical quality is acceptable.

Additional iterations provide diminishing returns.

Perfection is not the goal.

Stability is.

---

# Common Failure Modes

The refinement engine has failed when:

Every iteration changes too many things.

Identity drifts.

Different art styles emerge.

Prompt rewrites become inconsistent.

Regression is accepted.

The system chases novelty instead of improvement.

Iterations continue indefinitely without measurable progress.

The objective is convergence—not endless experimentation.

---

# Canonical Approval

A character becomes canonical only after passing every major evaluation category.

Approval requires:

✓ Identity preserved.

✓ Historical reconstruction complete.

✓ Artifact removal complete.

✓ Child appeal achieved.

✓ Universe consistency confirmed.

✓ Technical quality approved.

✓ Human review completed.

Canonical status should be rare.

Most images should remain work-in-progress until they genuinely satisfy the Identity Forge standards.

---

# Relationship to Director Desk

Director Desk should never receive experimental characters.

It receives only approved canonical actors.

Identity Forge absorbs uncertainty.

Director Desk receives certainty.

This separation ensures production stability.

---

# Success Criteria

The Refinement Engine succeeds when:

✓ Every iteration has a measurable purpose.

✓ Identity improves over time.

✓ Historical confidence increases.

✓ Visual quality converges.

✓ Child appeal improves.

✓ Canonical approval is earned rather than assumed.

✓ Every final character represents the strongest known reconstruction.

---

# Final Principle

Identity Forge rejects the myth of the perfect first prompt.

Historical reconstruction is a process of discovery.

Every refinement removes another layer of uncertainty.

Every critique sharpens understanding.

Every iteration brings the audience one step closer to meeting the real historical person.

The Refinement Engine exists to ensure that the final canonical character is not merely beautiful.

It is believable.

It is historically respectful.

It is emotionally memorable.

And it is ready to become a permanent member of the Identity Forge Universe.
