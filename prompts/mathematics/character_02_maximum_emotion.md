## Prompt

The eyes should be the focal point: clear catchlights, rich iris detail, a thoughtful and emotionally present gaze. The expression should communicate curiosity, intelligence, and approachability. Tactile fabric details. The viewer should feel invited into conversation with this historical figure.

No cold lighting. No blank expressions. No dead eyes. No engraving artifacts. No brush strokes. No canvas texture. No sepia. No black-and-white bias. No plastic skin. No wax figure appearance. No uncanny realism. No anime or cartoon styling.
