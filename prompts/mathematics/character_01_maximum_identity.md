## Inputs
1. Historical reference image of the mathematician
2. Mathematics environment image

Preserve every recognizable facial feature: skull shape, jawline, nose geometry, eye spacing, brow structure, hairline, beard, and age characteristics from the source. Three-quarter portrait, waist-up composition. Subsurface skin scattering. Natural hair texture. Period-accurate clothing with visible fabric weave. The identity from the reference must be immediately recognizable.

No engraving lines. No cross-hatching. No brush strokes. No canvas texture. No sepia. No black-and-white bias. No plastic skin. No wax figure appearance. No uncanny realism. No anime styling. No low-budget cartoon styling. No generic fantasy wizard appearance.
