# Mathematics Domain — Environment Prompt

## Inputs
- Used alone as the first Image 2 conditioning input.

## Prompt

A vast circular reading room filled with centuries of accumulated mathematical knowledge. Tall arched windows allow warm golden afternoon light to stream across worn oak tables. The architecture combines Romanesque stone arches with Renaissance wooden galleries rising three stories. Bookshelves line every wall, filled with leather-bound volumes, scrolls, and manuscripts from different eras. A large central chalkboard stands on a brass easel, covered with geometric diagrams and equations. Brass armillary spheres, compasses, set squares, and geometric models sit on side tables. The floor is worn stone, softened by faded Persian rugs. Dust motes float gently in shafts of sunlight. The space feels quiet, contemplative, and alive with centuries of thought. The color palette is warm oak, cream parchment, aged leather brown, soft brass gold, deep forest green, and navy. No people. No single historical figure. Timeless. Warm. Lived-in. Cinematic. The books show signs of handling. The chalkboard has partially erased markings. The room breathes.

No empty museum atmosphere. No cold library. No modern furniture. No fluorescent lighting. No sterile surfaces. No digital screens. No fantasy towers. No wizard clichés. No classroom setting.
