# Identity Forge Mission

Identity Forge exists to create a canonical cast of 100 historically grounded, emotionally compelling characters that become the foundation for our films.

Our success is measured by one metric: **approved canonical characters completed**.

We embrace the 80/20 principle. The goal is not perfection—it is consistent excellence delivered quickly. A character that is historically faithful, visually cohesive, emotionally engaging, and production-ready today is more valuable than a theoretically perfect character that delays the entire project.

Every decision must answer one question:

**Does this help us create the next approved canonical character?**

If not, it belongs in Version 2.

When faced with two acceptable choices, choose the one that gets the next canonical character approved sooner.

The Bible provides our standards; it is now locked. We will not pause production to endlessly refine the system. We will improve through making.

Each completed character is another step toward our real objective: creating unforgettable educational films that allow children to meet history's greatest minds as living, relatable people.

Great is better than perfect if it gets us there in one-fifth the time.

Build the cast.

Finish the 100.

Then make the movies.
